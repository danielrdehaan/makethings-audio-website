Privacy

This covers makethings.audio and Make Things plugins, run by [Company name]. StemShare has its own privacy policy. We don't sell your data, and this site has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Your audio

The plugins process audio on your computer. Your audio isn't sent to us.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, name and password (stored hashed by our sign-in provider).
  • Your computers: for each computer you sign in on, its name, when it was last seen, and a hashed install id, so you can see and remove them and we can keep to the limit of 3.
  • A hashed hardware id: a salted hash of your computer's hardware id, used only so that each computer gets one free trial. We can't get the original id back from it.
  • What you have access to: your plugins, trials, and any group or grant that gives you access.
  • Purchases: Paddle takes your payment. We never see your card. Paddle sends us your customer and subscription ids, what you bought, its status and your receipts.
  • If you ask us something: what you send. An education request includes your name, email address, school and any details you add, and comes to us by email.
  • If you ask to hear when the plugins go on sale: your email address, used only for that.
  • Shared Modular patches: if you share a patch, the patch itself.

When the plugins go online

Plugins go online to renew your licence, to check for updates, and for Modular's shared patches. Nothing else.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase: sign-in and database.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, downloads, and the bot check on our forms (Turnstile).
  • Paddle: checkout, tax and billing. Paddle is the seller, so it handles your payment details under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend: sending email.
  • Google: our email inbox.

Browser storage

When you sign in, your browser keeps your session in this site's local storage. It isn't used to track you. The bot check and Paddle's checkout may set what they need to work.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it, which you can do from your account page. We keep billing records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. We delete the on-sale list once it has been used.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, use your account page or email us. You can remove a computer from your account at any time.
