What the plugins add to the rack.

  • Your plugins, inside the patchLoad an AU or VST3 instrument or effect as a module, and modulate its knobs like any other.
  • In time with your songHost Transport turns your DAW’s play, bar and tempo into voltages, and synced modulators lock to the bar.
  • Macros your DAW automatesMacro 1 to 8 appear in your DAW. Automate one and its card moves the knobs it’s routed to.
  • Modulators that hear other tracksOn a Mac, a follower or envelope can listen to another track’s MIDI or audio, even one band of a Make Things EQ.
  • Notes on the gridWrite notes beside the modules, with links that light up the knob or jack they’re about.
  • One engine everywhereThe web lab runs the plugin’s own engine, so a patch sounds the same in your browser and in your DAW.
