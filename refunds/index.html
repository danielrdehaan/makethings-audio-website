Refunds

You can get a full refund within 14 days of a payment, no questions asked. This covers buying a plugin and any subscription payment.

How to ask

Email support@makethings.audio from the address on your account. Paddle, which sells the plugins for us, sends the money back to your original payment method.

What happens next

A refund ends the access it paid for. For a subscription, cancel as well if you don't want it to renew.

Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time from your account page. You keep access to the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again. Cancelling on its own isn't a refund.

After 14 days

Email us anyway if something went wrong, like a duplicate charge. Nothing here limits rights the law gives you.
